# Runbook exercises (`NFR-9`)

A runbook is exercised when a person follows it on a running test deployment and records the result here. The line says
the date, the runbook id, who ran it, the environment, and what differed from the text (and the fix). A runbook that no
line names is not exercised, whatever tests cover the behaviour under it.
