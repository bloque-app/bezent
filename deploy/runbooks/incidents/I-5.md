# I-5

## What it is

A shortfall treated as failure: a balance shortfall was classed as terminal and left customers debited and unpaid, and a
fee account ran dry unnoticed (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: after acceptance, a shortfall in reserve, fee position or mint window is a named, parked condition that
resumes by itself, with one alert per position, and never a failure or a refund (`REQ-LM-3`, `REQ-OT-5`). The conditions are
`CND-ReserveShort`, `CND-FeeShort` and `CND-MintWindow`; the tests above show each holding and resuming. Residual: the transfer
waits until the position is mended. A gap, stated plainly: no service reads a delivery account's balance yet, so a fee position
running dry shows only once a transfer is held in `CND-FeeShort` (`fee_short_transfers`; `crates/store/migrations/0090`, F-014 NOTES).

## Pages

`reserve-alert` (`../pages/reserve.md`) when the reserve or its surplus is below its alert level. See
`../conditions/CND-ReserveShort.md`, `../conditions/CND-FeeShort.md`, `../conditions/CND-MintWindow.md`.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT metric, asset, network, direction, value FROM ops_observables()
 WHERE metric IN ('fee_short_transfers', 'reserve_amount', 'reserve_equation_outcome', 'nonfinal_transfers')
 ORDER BY metric, asset, network;
```

A value of `fee_short_transfers` above zero is the only fee signal the store has. A NULL `reserve_amount` is "not known", never zero (`REQ-NX-3`).

## Do

- Mend the position outside the store, from the deployment's own notes (top up the fee float, restore the reserve, wait for the
  window), then record it the same day with `operator_record` (kind `float-top-up` or `surplus-contribution`, naming what and why).
- Do nothing to the held transfers: they resume by themselves.
- Pause (`operator_pause`) a route if new requests would only join the queue while the cause is unclear.

## Never

- No failure or refund on a shortfall, a timer or an unknown (`REQ-LM-3`, `REQ-RF-1`).
- No bypass of the hold by editing a transfer's state or condition (`REQ-GV-5`).
- No operator top-up bound to a deposit or minted: it is never an arrival to bind (test `inv_1_inv_2_req_in_3_mf1_an_operator_top_up_is_never_bound_nor_minted`, `services/core/tests/reserve_check.rs`).
