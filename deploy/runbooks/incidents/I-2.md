# I-2

## What it is

Non-execution never provable: transactions signed without an expiry, so "not yet" and "never" looked alike, and a nonce
released on a timer was reused (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: `REQ-NX-1` makes non-execution provable in bounded time. On the issuing network a transaction expires with
its era, and one whose era passed is never signed (`REQ-HN-3`); the proof is the expired window at a final block with the nonce
unconsumed. On an origin network, where nothing expires, the core orders a cancel at the deadline that consumes the nonce
(`REQ-NX-4`); the proof is the nonce consumed by a different transaction at a final block. Time, silence and a "not found" prove nothing
(`REQ-NX-3`), and a nonce consumed by a transaction bezent did not sign raises the compromise path. Residual: a cancel that stays unknown.

## Pages

`key-compromise` (`../pages/key-compromise.md`) when a consumer of the nonce is not one bezent recorded.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT metric, asset, network, direction, value FROM ops_observables()
 WHERE metric IN ('unknown_legs', 'unknown_legs_oldest_age_seconds', 'reviews_open');
SELECT transfer_id, condition, state_before_review, entered_at, due_at FROM reviews ORDER BY entered_at;
```

The attempt's signed transactions, cancel and proofs are in tables the operator login cannot read; use the requester-facing
status or the deployment's own notes. The proof is public chain data (`REQ-NX-2`).

## Do

- Leave the review open. The core attaches the cancel's final proof to it by itself (`review-evidence`, F-003 NOTES 20); a new
  attempt or the refund mint then needs a resolution naming that final block and a second approver (`REQ-RV-2`, `REQ-GV-5`).
- If the cancel stays unknown, nothing is refunded and the review holds (F-003 NOTES 20).
- On the `key-compromise` page: pause (`operator_pause`), then follow `../pages/key-compromise.md`; record each act done elsewhere with `operator_record`.

## Never

- No nonce released or reused on a timer; no hand-built transaction on the delivery key (`REQ-NX-3`, `INV-9`).
- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`); no decision on a non-final read (`INV-16`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on transfers, transitions or proofs (`REQ-GV-5`).
