# I-6

## What it is

Skipped blocks: the follower read only the newest final block, finality advances in bursts, and a missed event hid a
delivery that the chain had made (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: observation is complete over contiguous ranges. An endpoint does not move past a range it failed to read;
it records the range as a visible gap and fills it from history before anything after it (`REQ-OB-3`, `REQ-RN-6`). The store
refuses a range past the watermark, and two providers that disagree on a range record a gap, never an arrival. A held
transfer looks like this: `open_gaps` above zero or `observer_lag_estimate_seconds` growing for a network, with transfers waiting on it.

## Pages

No page kind for a gap itself is established in the code; the signals are `open_gaps` and `observer_lag_estimate_seconds`. A reserve
check that cannot read pages as `reserve-check-stalled` or `reserve-check-incomplete` (`../pages/reserve.md`).

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT network, metric, value FROM ops_observables() WHERE metric IN ('open_gaps', 'observer_lag_estimate_seconds') ORDER BY network;
SELECT metric, asset, network, value FROM ops_observables() WHERE metric IN ('unknown_legs', 'nonfinal_transfers');
```

A NULL `observer_lag_estimate_seconds` means the network has never recorded a final head: not known, not zero (`REQ-NX-3`). The gap rows themselves
(`observation_gaps`) are not readable by the operator login; take them from the deployment's own notes.

## Do

- Let the endpoint fill the gap. A persistent gap or provider disagreement is not mended by replacing the task; act on the
  alert (`deploy/health.md`). The cause (providers, history source) is dealt with from the deployment's own notes.
- Pause (`operator_pause`) the network's routes if the cause is unexplained (`REQ-IC-2`); in-flight work keeps being observed.
- If a transfer reaches review because its leg went unseen, resolve it by the leg's final coordinate from public chain data
  (`manual_actions`, a second approver where value moves), as in `I-1.md`.

## Never

- No gap marked filled, and no observation inserted by hand (`REQ-GV-5`, `INV-10`); no skipping to the newest block.
- No payout booked failed because an event was missed: unseen is unknown (`REQ-NX-3`, `REQ-RF-1`).
- No decision on a non-final read (`INV-16`).
