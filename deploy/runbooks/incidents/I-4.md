# I-4

## What it is

"Confirmed" when the message left: a cross-chain transfer was marked confirmed on dispatch, the next step failed seconds
later, and the funds arrived later still (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: nothing is confirmed on dispatch. Nothing is minted before the arrival is final and nothing is paid before
the redemption is final (`INV-6`, `INV-7`, `REQ-OT-3`). `Delivered` means the leg was included; `Completed` needs the
destination leg final and its execution result showing the full amount moved (`REQ-TR-3`). Included, final and completed
are three conditions, each with its own time (`REQ-TR-4`), and a status carries observations, not verdicts (`REQ-ST-3`).
Delivery to another network is the requester's, from what it receives (`REQ-OT-4`); the second hop is not bezent's to confirm.
Residual: a transfer that stays in `Delivered` because the leg is not final yet.

## Pages

None. A transfer waiting on finality is ordinary; its age is the signal.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT asset, network, direction, value FROM ops_observables()
 WHERE metric IN ('oldest_nonfinal_transfer_age_seconds', 'nonfinal_transfers') ORDER BY value DESC;
SELECT metric, network, value FROM ops_observables() WHERE metric IN ('observer_lag_seconds', 'open_gaps');
```

Which state and which milestones a transfer has comes from the requester-facing status; the operator login cannot read `transfers`.

## Do

- Wait. A leg not yet final is not decided on (`INV-16`). If the observer lags or has a gap, treat it as `I-6.md`.
- If the transfer reaches `Needs-Review` (partial execution, another amount), treat it as `I-8.md` and `I-13.md`.
- If the cause of a long age is not understood: pause (`operator_pause`); in-flight work keeps being observed (`REQ-GV-1`).

## Never

- No state change on dispatch, inclusion alone or a node's reply; no decision on a non-final read (`INV-16`).
- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on transfers, transitions or milestones (`REQ-GV-5`, `REQ-TR-2`).
