# I-10

## What it is

One stuck transfer stops everything: a lost observation held a nonce and the signing queue behind it stalled for hours
(`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: it declares the signing lane and keeps order within it by nonce (`REQ-EO-4`); a retry is gated per
on-chain reference, not per sender (`attempt_gate_open`, migration 0017). A delivery with no leg seen goes to review at its
deadline and, on an origin network, a cancel consuming the nonce is ordered at the same time (`REQ-NX-4`, `REQ-RV-1`); a
preparation refused with no named condition also reaches review at the deadline (F-003 NOTES 18, 24). Not established in the
code: that an unknown transfer never delays later transfers of the same sender; no test says so. Not covered: an attempt whose
preparation never happens at all because the endpoint is down has no deadline edge (F-003 NOTES 24, issue #48).

## Pages

`payout-refusal-alert` and `payout-window-alert` (`../pages/payout.md`); see `../conditions/CND-PayoutWindow.md`.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT asset, network, direction, metric, value FROM ops_observables()
 WHERE metric IN ('oldest_nonfinal_transfer_age_seconds', 'unknown_legs_oldest_age_seconds', 'reviews_oldest_age_seconds')
 ORDER BY value DESC;
SELECT network, value FROM ops_observables() WHERE metric = 'observer_lag_seconds';
```

An old age on one route with other routes at zero points to one stuck transfer; an endpoint that has stopped beating
shows in its health check (`deploy/health.md`).

## Do

- Let the deadline take the stuck transfer to review; then resolve it by a final coordinate (`manual_actions`, second
  approver where value moves), as in `I-1.md`. That frees nothing by hand; the cancel's proof frees the nonce.
- If the cause is a down endpoint, restore it from the deployment's own notes; an unhealthy task needs the alert, not restarts.
- Pause (`operator_pause`) the route to stop new work joining the queue; in-flight work continues (`REQ-GV-1`).

## Never

- No nonce skipped or freed by hand, and no transaction signed outside an intent (`INV-9`).
- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on transfers, intents or signed transactions (`REQ-GV-5`).

## Exercise

Drill on a test network: leave one payout unobserved and send others on the same route; confirm the stuck one is reviewed
and cancelled at its deadline and note what the others did meanwhile. Record who, when and what was found here.

Status: not exercised (NFR-9). Behaviour proven by the tests listed above, not by a run of this runbook.
