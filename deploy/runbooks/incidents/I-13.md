# I-13

## What it is

Amount residue: a hold was taken for the order amount, the chain moved a slightly different one, and the difference sat
unseen for weeks (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: every amount is an exact integer count of minor units (`INV-11`). The delivered amount equals the
transfer's amount and nothing is deducted: bezent charges no fee and costs fall on surplus (`REQ-TR-5`, `REQ-BK-4`). A mint
order rests on one final arrival and its amount (`REQ-IN-4`); a deposit of a token with more precision mints the floor and the
exact remainder is booked to surplus with the deposit's reference (`REQ-AS-9`). A delivery of any other amount holds
`CND-AmountMismatch`, reports both amounts, and goes to `Needs-Review`. A residue that does reach the book diverges the reserve
equation, which is evaluated every minute per asset at zero tolerance (`NFR-5`). Residual: that review, or that divergence.

## Pages

`reserve-divergence`, `reserve-alert` (`../pages/reserve.md`); `../conditions/CND-AmountMismatch.md`.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT asset, outcome, reason, differences, evaluated_at FROM reserve_evaluations ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 5;
SELECT transfer_id, asset, condition, state_before_review, entered_at FROM reviews WHERE condition = 'CND-AmountMismatch';
SELECT metric, asset, network, value FROM ops_observables() WHERE metric IN ('reserve_equation_outcome', 'reserve_amount');
```

`differences` itemises each term that disagrees. Outcome 1 is a divergence; 2 is "could not be made".

## Do

- Read both amounts from the review and the chain, and match by the on-chain reference only (`REQ-ID-2`, `REQ-OT-2`).
- Resolve the review by attaching the final coordinate of the leg (`manual_actions`; a `manual_approvals` row by another
  login where value moves, `REQ-RV-2`). A residue owed to a person is settled by an ordinary transfer, never by a book edit.
- A divergence pauses the asset's routes by itself; lift only with a second person (`operator_request_unpause`, then
  `operator_approve_unpause` by a different login). Record any movement made outside the store with `operator_record`.

## Never

- No amount or time used to match a leg to a transfer (`REQ-OT-2`); no rounding or fee taken out of a delivery (`REQ-TR-5`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on the book, transfers or readings: a correction is a new entry made by the core (`INV-17`, `REQ-GV-5`).
- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`).
