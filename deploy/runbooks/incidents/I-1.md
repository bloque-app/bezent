# I-1

## What it is

Failure reported, then paid: a cash-out was told to a partner as failed, later reached success, and the partner had
already refunded its users (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: it has no failure on an unknown. A leg that is not seen, or not final, is unknown and past its deadline
goes to `Needs-Review` with an owner (`REQ-RV-1`); a refund needs the leg proven not executed with the proof final
(`REQ-RF-1`, `INV-8`), and a status carries observations, not verdicts (`REQ-ST-3`). Tests above. In an incident, in-flight
transfers are reported unknown or delayed (`REQ-IC-3`). The residual case is a transfer held in review, or a refund held back by an inclusion.

## Pages

`resolution-blocked-by-inclusion`: a refund or new attempt was withheld because a payout inclusion stands
(`../pages/payout.md`). A held transfer raises no other page by itself.

## Confirm

The operator login reads a transfer through `transfer_status` and the sequenced log through `event_log`; it cannot read
`transfers` or `transitions` themselves (`crates/store/src/grants.rs`).

```sql
SELECT transfer_id, kind, asset, condition, state_before_review, entered_at, owner, due_at FROM reviews ORDER BY entered_at;
SELECT metric, asset, network, direction, value FROM ops_observables()
 WHERE metric IN ('unknown_legs', 'unknown_legs_oldest_age_seconds', 'reviews_open', 'reviews_oldest_age_seconds');
```

## Do

- Tell the requester the transfer is unknown or delayed, never failed. Say it outside the store, from the deployment's own notes.
- Leave the review open until a final coordinate settles it. Then resolve it: `manual_actions` (kind `review-resolution`,
  `to_state`, the coordinate) and, for a resolution into `Source-Final`, `Delivering` or `Refunding`, a `manual_approvals`
  row by a different login (`REQ-RV-2`, `REQ-GV-5`; F-003 NOTES 6). The core verifies the coordinate before it runs the edge.
- If a refund is withheld by a standing inclusion, wait: it runs by itself once the inclusion is withdrawn, and a final inclusion closes the review as `Delivered` only by a recorded resolution (F-003 NOTES 26).
- Pause (`operator_pause`) while the cause is not understood; a pause stops new work only (`REQ-GV-1`).

## Never

- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`); no word of "failed" to a requester while a leg is unknown (`REQ-IC-3`).
- No decision on a non-final read (`INV-16`); no "could not look" read as "did not happen" (`REQ-NX-3`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on transfers, transitions, observations or the book (`REQ-GV-5`).
