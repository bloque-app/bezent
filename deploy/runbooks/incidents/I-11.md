# I-11

## What it is

Silent outcomes: quarantined operations left no record the ledger could see, and success on chain produced no notice
(`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: every state change and condition is appended to a gapless, sequenced log that a requester reads from its
last position without any subscription (`REQ-ST-4`, `REQ-ST-2`); notifications are at-least-once and signed, a delivery that
exhausts its retries is dead-lettered and the log still has the entry, and no requester needs a notification (`REQ-ST-5`).
A held transfer has an owner and a one-business-day service level (`REQ-RV-1`); an observation the book cannot post is
quarantined once, paged and pauses its asset. Residual: a dead or pending notification, a review past its due time.

## Pages

`book-quarantine` (`../pages/reserve.md`).

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT metric, value FROM ops_observables()
 WHERE metric IN ('undelivered_notifications', 'undelivered_notifications_oldest_age_seconds', 'dead_notifications',
                  'reviews_open', 'reviews_oldest_age_seconds');
SELECT transfer_id, condition, owner, entered_at, due_at FROM reviews WHERE due_at < now() ORDER BY due_at;
SELECT id, observation_id, asset, reason, recorded_at FROM book_quarantine ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 10;
```

The webhook tables are the dispatcher's alone; the operator login sees only the counts and ages.

## Do

- A pending or dead notification is not an outage of record: the requester reads the log from its last position (`REQ-ST-5`).
  Mend the callback or the dispatcher from the deployment's own notes; a requester replaces its own callback URL.
- A review past `due_at` is the operations function's to resolve: attach a final coordinate (`manual_actions`, second
  approver where value moves), as in `I-1.md`.
- A quarantined observation pauses its asset; lift only with a second person (`operator_request_unpause`, then
  `operator_approve_unpause`).

## Never

- No silent decision: nothing is resolved or paused without the acting login and its justification recorded (`REQ-GV-5`).
- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on the event log, the book or the quarantine (`INV-17`, `REQ-GV-5`).
