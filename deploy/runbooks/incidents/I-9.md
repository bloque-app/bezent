# I-9

## What it is

Decisions on unfinalised reads: a confirmation from the best block raced a check at the final block and many accounts were
stuck (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: every read that decides a disposition is made at a final block (`REQ-OB-5`). The endpoints read nothing
above the final head, an arrival counts as final only when the final point has passed its block and the hash still matches
(`REQ-OB-2`, `REQ-IN-2`), and the store refuses `Source-Final` for an arrival that is not final or was reorganised away.
An observation that stops matching before finality is withdrawn and anything waiting on it stops (`REQ-IN-7`, `CND-Withdrawn`).
A refund or new attempt is withheld, not decided, while a payout inclusion stands (F-003 NOTES 26). Residual: transfers
waiting on finality, or withheld. The SPEC asks that a final destination leg be looked for before a source leg is declared
unknown (`REQ-OB-5`, SHOULD); that this is built is not established in the code.

## Pages

`resolution-blocked-by-inclusion` (`../pages/payout.md`); see `../conditions/CND-Withdrawn.md`.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT asset, network, direction, value FROM ops_observables()
 WHERE metric IN ('nonfinal_transfers', 'oldest_nonfinal_transfer_age_seconds', 'unknown_legs') ORDER BY metric;
SELECT transfer_id, condition, state_before_review, entered_at FROM reviews WHERE condition = 'CND-Withdrawn';
```

## Do

- Wait for finality; the core decides only at a final block. A withheld refund or attempt runs by itself once the inclusion is
  withdrawn, and a final inclusion closes the review as `Delivered` only by a recorded resolution naming it (F-003 NOTES 26).
- To resolve a review, attach the final coordinate (`manual_actions`; a second approver in `manual_approvals` where value
  moves), as in `I-1.md`.
- Pause (`operator_pause`) if finality is stalled and the cause is not understood.

## Never

- No decision on a non-final read (`INV-16`); no mint or payout before the leg is final (`INV-6`, `INV-7`).
- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on transfers, transitions or observations (`REQ-GV-5`).
