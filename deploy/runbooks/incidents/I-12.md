# I-12

## What it is

Timestamps that can be rewritten: a workflow clock changed when a step was re-paused by hand, so one latency query gave
very different answers, no block number was stored, and many rows carried the epoch (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5,
"Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: every state change records the coordinate it rests on (block number, hash and time) and the time bezent saw
it, and no recorded time is overwritten or recomputed (`REQ-TR-2`); transitions are append-only and milestones are written only
by their own transition, in the store, by trigger (`INV-5`). Included, final and completed carry separate times (`REQ-TR-4`).
Latency comes only from write-once chain milestones (`NFR-3`), and a restore re-derives every view from durable records (`NFR-7`).
A pause and its lift are stamped with the store's clock and the session's login, append-only. There is no manual re-pause that
rewrites a clock. Not established in the code: a weekly latency report (`NFR-4`).

## Pages

None. There is no live signal for a rewritten time; the guard is that it cannot be written.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT id, kind, subject, actor, recorded_at FROM admin_actions ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 20;
SELECT transfer_id, entered_at, due_at FROM reviews ORDER BY entered_at LIMIT 20;
```

The ages in `ops_observables()` count from the time bezent recorded a row; the chain times of a transfer are in the
requester-facing status. The operator login cannot read `transitions`; take any other check from the deployment's own notes.

## Do

- Answer a latency question from the milestones and their coordinates in the status, not from a row's insertion time.
- If a recorded time looks wrong, do not repair it: report it as a defect from the deployment's own notes, with the
  evidence (block number, hash); a store that allowed the change would be broken.

## Never

- No UPDATE or INSERT on transfers, transitions, milestones or observations (`REQ-GV-5`, `REQ-TR-2`, `INV-5`).
- No time recomputed or back-filled to improve a figure; no latency counted from a time bezent could rewrite (`NFR-3`).
- No refund or failure on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-RF-1`, `REQ-RV-1`).
