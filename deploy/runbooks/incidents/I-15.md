# I-15

## What it is

Limits found after value moved, fixed by hand: a withdrawal below an exchange's minimum failed after earlier legs had
executed, the payout was completed out of band and the records were repaired by inserting rows (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md`
§5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: a route declares its limits and bezent refuses at request time, with a named error and before any value
moves, whatever it can already tell will not execute (`REQ-LM-1`, `REQ-LM-2`). A deposit mints and a withdrawal pays out
(`REQ-IN-4`, `REQ-OT-1`); there is no earlier leg left stranded by a later one. A limit it cannot tell in advance (a payout
dry run refused by the reserve's outflow allowance) is held as `CND-PayoutWindow`, never failed or refunded, and resumes by itself
(`REQ-LM-3`). A refusal that names nothing reaches review at the deadline. Residual: that hold or that review.

## Pages

`payout-window-alert` (a hold longer than its bound) and `payout-refusal-alert` (repeated refusals of one attempt), both in
`../pages/payout.md`; see `../conditions/CND-PayoutWindow.md`.

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT transfer_id, kind, asset, condition, state_before_review, entered_at, due_at FROM reviews ORDER BY entered_at;
SELECT metric, asset, network, direction, value FROM ops_observables() WHERE metric IN ('nonfinal_transfers', 'reviews_open');
```

## Do

- A payout completed out of band is NOT repaired by inserting rows. There is no such path (`REQ-GV-5`): the store grants an
  operator no write to transfers, transitions, the book or observations (tests above). The ordinary path is a review
  resolution that attaches the coordinate of the ordinary edge (`manual_actions`, kind `review-resolution`, and a
  `manual_approvals` row by a different login where value moves, `REQ-RV-2`); the core verifies the coordinate against what the
  endpoints recorded at a final block. Whether a hand-made payout, which carries no bezent reference, can be such evidence is
  not established in the code; if it cannot, the review holds and nothing is refunded.
- Record the out-of-band act itself with `operator_record` the same day (what, why, the coordinate).
- Wait out `CND-PayoutWindow`; it resumes by itself when the allowance refills.

## Never

- No INSERT or UPDATE on transfers, transitions, the book or observations to make records agree with a hand-run act (`REQ-GV-5`).
- No refund or failure because a limit was hit, on a timer or an unknown (`REQ-LM-3`, `REQ-RF-1`).
- No value moved outside the ordinary steps (`REQ-GV-5`, `INV-14`).
