# I-8

## What it is

Inclusion taken for execution: a cross-chain message's local outcome was logged and never checked, so a partial execution
could pass as success (`notes/BRIDGE-REQUIREMENTS.md` §5, "Bloque's internal incident records").

What bezent does: it checks the execution result of every transaction it observes, not only inclusion (`REQ-OB-6`); an
included but failed payout is recorded as not executed, and only that proof, once final, can open a refund (`REQ-NX-1`).
A transfer moves from `Delivered` to `Completed` only when the leg is final and its result shows the full amount moved to the
destination (`REQ-TR-3`); a leg to another recipient is not executed in full, and a delivery of another amount holds
`CND-AmountMismatch` with both amounts and goes to `Needs-Review` (`REQ-TR-5`). Residual: that review.

## Pages

None. The held transfer shows as a review (`../conditions/CND-AmountMismatch.md`).

## Confirm

```sql
SELECT transfer_id, kind, asset, condition, state_before_review, entered_at, due_at FROM reviews
 WHERE condition = 'CND-AmountMismatch' OR state_before_review = 'Delivered' ORDER BY entered_at;
SELECT metric, value FROM ops_observables() WHERE metric IN ('reviews_open', 'reviews_oldest_age_seconds');
```

Both amounts and the leg's result are in the requester-facing status and on chain; the operator login cannot read the observations.

## Do

- Read the leg's execution result from public chain data and decide from a final coordinate only.
- Resolve the review by attaching that coordinate (`manual_actions`, kind `review-resolution`); a resolution that moves value
  (into `Delivering` or `Refunding`) needs a `manual_approvals` row by a different login (`REQ-RV-2`, `REQ-GV-5`).
- The difference of a partial delivery is not repaired by hand: see `I-13.md`.
- After a stall recovery, the transfer's delivery deadline still runs from its source time, so a recovered transfer goes
  `Delivering` to `Needs-Review` at once, even though its payout lands. Close that review with one `review-resolution` to
  `Delivered`, citing the final payout coordinate. It moves no value, so it needs no second approver. Precedent: transfer 11
  in production, 2026-11-13.

## Never

- No inclusion treated as success, and no success from the sender's own log (`REQ-OB-6`, `REQ-AR-2`).
- No refund before the proof of non-execution is final (`REQ-RF-1`); no decision on a non-final read (`INV-16`).
- No UPDATE or INSERT on transfers, transitions or milestones (`REQ-GV-5`).

completed and the second sits in review with both amounts reported. Record who, when and what was found here.
