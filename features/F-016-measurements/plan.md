Measures the figures `DEC-17` needs. The volume half reads the requester's production orders with read-only access and
nothing that writes; its report is kept privately by Bloque. The rest of `F-016` (route p99 targets and ceilings,
`NFR-2`) is measured on the test bed and recorded here too. This work does not edit `SPEC.md` or `PLAN.md`.
