Amendment 0001 — one core with backends, the Kreivo contract, redeem, and the owner's rulings to 2026-10-11

Status: ruled by the owner on the decision form ("USDB Bridge Decisions") and in chat, 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-11; to be applied to SPEC.md v0.3.0 Target: SPEC.md v0.2.0 Date: 2026-10-11 Rationale in one line: the bridge becomes one core with a backend per asset class, each bridged asset has one reserve, the issuing-network end is a contract in a dedicated community that mints to an order's destination and burns on redemption, and fiat-backed assets are bridged through attested backends.

Every item cites the decision-form items that ruled it (the form is kept privately). Identifiers are never renumbered: a withdrawn identifier stays withdrawn.

A1 — One core, a backend per asset class (gen-core)

  • §0 "Networks" becomes "Networks and backends". The bridge connects one issuing network with backends. A backend is the counterpart system for one class of origin: a network, or a financial rail reached through a named party. Each backend declares an assurance class (A2).
  • §4.4(b) is retitled "Backends". REQ-RN-1–REQ-RN-6 keep their text and bind network backends. Add:
    • REQ-BE-1 Each backend MUST implement the backend contract. It MUST observe an arrival with a stable identifier and its finality, deliver idempotently, give evidence of non-execution, and report the reserve it holds.
    • REQ-BE-2 Each backend MUST declare its assurance class. It is public when every observation, reserve balance and proof of non-execution can be checked from public data. It is attested when they are taken from a named party's reports.
    • REQ-BE-3 The statement (REQ-PR-1) MUST name each bridged asset's backend and its assurance class.
    • REQ-BE-4 REQ-BK-6, REQ-NX-2 and REQ-ST-1 bind public backends. For an attested backend, the bridge MUST record each report of the attesting party with that party's own identifiers, and the statement MUST say that the asset's reserve is attested.
  • Glossary: add Backend and Assurance class. "Origin network" is kept, as "a network backend".
  • DEF-3 (other kinds of origin network) is withdrawn: those kinds are now in scope as backends.

A2 — Attested backends take the ramp's reports (gen-bank-evidence, br-asset-names)

  • Add REQ-BE-5. For an attested backend whose attesting party is a requester, that requester's report of an arrival, a payout or a reserve balance is the observation. REQ-RQ-7 and REQ-AR-2 ("no caller asserts an outcome") bind public backends only.
  • Add a "Trade-off, stated plainly" callout after REQ-BE-5: for an asset with an attested backend, the attesting requester can cause a mint. What bounds it is the on-chain component's mint window and supply ceiling (§7.F), and the statement, which names the party whose word backs the asset.
  • Notes (not SPEC.md): COPM, MXNB and BRLB are backed through Swaps, an attested backend.

A3 — One reserve per bridged asset (br-one-reserve, br-asset-names)

  • Add REQ-AS-8. Each bridged asset MUST have exactly one reserve backend. For a network backend, the reserve sits on exactly one network. An attested backend MAY hold it in several positions (accounts), each itemised (REQ-BK-5).
  • §1.7 assumption 3 becomes: "One bridged asset per origin token family, reserved on one backend. Deliveries to any other network go through a protocol venue, straight to the beneficiary (A10). Tokens of different issuers are never merged (REQ-AS-3)."
  • REQ-OT-4 becomes: "A withdrawal MUST be paid from the asset's reserve, directly on the reserve network, or through a protocol venue that delivers straight to the beneficiary on another network."
  • REQ-OT-5 keeps its text, with "that network's treasury" replaced by "the asset's reserve".
  • §5.7 is retitled "Venues", and REQ-RB-1–REQ-RB-10 are withdrawn: with one reserve per asset there is nothing to rebalance. Add REQ-VN-6. A delivery through a protocol venue is a payout like any other. The bridge signs the leg out of the reserve. The venue's delivery to the beneficiary is its destination leg, observed at finality. A step that anyone may perform MAY be driven by the requester.
  • US-F1 and US-F2 and their criteria are withdrawn. Epic F becomes "Venues", with US-F3: "As a requester, I pay a withdrawal on a network other than the asset's reserve network."
    • AC-F3.1 Given a withdrawal to another network, Then the bridge pays it through a protocol venue, straight to the beneficiary (REQ-OT-4, REQ-VN-6).
    • AC-F3.2 Given the venue's delivery is final, Then the withdrawal is Completed.
  • OQ-1 is closed by this item.
  • Notes: bUSDC is reserved on Polygon, bEURC on Base, and bBTC on BNB Chain (as BTCB, br-bbtc-backing).

A4 — Mint only on the backend's finality signal (gen-reversal, the owner's note)

  • Add REQ-OB-7. A mint MUST rest on the backend's own finality signal. For an attested backend, that is the party's report that the payment is final under its rail's rules. The party MUST NOT report a payment final before its rail guarantees it.
  • Add OQ-14: "What happens when a payment an attested backend reported final is reversed afterwards?" It blocks REQ-OB-7 and §5.3. gen-reversal stays open for discussion.

A5 — The issuing-network end is a contract in a dedicated community (br-contract-512, superseding br-issuer)

  • REQ-AR-4 keeps its text: mint and destroy authority stays chain-enforced. The relayer account as issuer (br-issuer, 2026-10-04) is not applied.
  • Add REQ-HN-10. The on-chain component's mint and destroy authority MUST be held through a group dedicated to it. No other on-chain program, and no other holder, may exercise that authority. That group's governance is the upgrade authority of REQ-GV-3.
  • OQ-5 is closed by this item (who governs: the dedicated group's governance).

A6 — Mint to the order's destination; redeem by anyone, burnt at once (br-redeem-burn, the owner's design of 2026-10-11)

  • §7.F's table is replaced by:

    Capability Who may call Refuses when
    Mint an amount of a bridged asset to the address named in the order, with an on-chain reference The delivery role The reference was used before; the amount exceeds the per-transfer bound, the mint window or the supply ceiling; the asset or direction is paused
    Redeem: take an amount of a bridged asset from the caller and destroy it in the same call, recording the backend, the payout instructions or their commitment (A7) and a reference Anyone The asset or direction is paused; the amount is below the route's minimum; the instructions do not fit the backend
    Refund mint: mint, once, to the redeemer of a redemption whose payout is proven not executed, tied to that redemption's reference The delivery role That redemption was refunded before; the mint window would be exceeded
    Pause / unpause per asset, per direction, or all The pause role / governance —
    Set bounds: supply ceiling, mint window, per-transfer maximum Governance (raising), the pause role (lowering) A raise without the delay of REQ-GV-3

    The rows "Mint into a ledger", "Escrow call", "Record escrow", "Destroy" and "Return" are withdrawn.

  • REQ-HN-8 becomes: "The on-chain component MUST NOT destroy value held by any account but its own. A redemption takes the value into the component and destroys it there."

  • REQ-OT-1 becomes: "A withdrawal is funded by a redemption on the issuing network, and only by one." REQ-OT-2 becomes: "A withdrawal MUST be bound to its redemption by the redemption's on-chain reference. It MUST NOT be matched by amount, sender or time." REQ-OT-7 and REQ-OT-8 are withdrawn: a redemption is the withdrawal's request.

  • REQ-OT-3 becomes: "The bridge MUST NOT submit a payout before the redemption is final."

  • REQ-OT-6 becomes: "The redeemed value is destroyed at redemption. If every payout attempt is proven not executed at finality, the redeemer is refunded by one refund mint tied to that redemption."

  • REQ-RF-4 becomes: "A withdrawal's refund is a refund mint to the redeemer, tied to the redemption's reference. It counts against the mint window."

  • INV-7 becomes: "Nothing is paid out before its redemption is final." (Service)

  • INV-8 becomes: "No refund mint is made unless every payout attempt for the redemption is proven not executed at finality, and at most one is made per redemption." (Service + chain)

  • §5.3: the term Undestroyed is replaced by OwedPayout, the sum of final redemptions whose payout is not yet final. The equation becomes:

    Σ Reserve(A) + InTransit(A) = Supply(A) + OwedMint(A) + OwedPayout(A) + Surplus(A)

    REQ-BK-2 lists OwedPayout in place of Undestroyed. InTransit is now value moving through a protocol venue.

  • Add a "Trade-off, stated plainly" callout in §5.6: burning at once means a failed payout is made whole by the mint authority. A refund mint is bounded by the mint window and is one per redemption, so a stolen delivery key cannot use refunds to exceed the window.

  • US-B1's criteria: AC-B1.1 becomes "Given a redemption, Then nothing is paid out before it is final (REQ-OT-3, INV-7)". AC-B1.2 becomes "Given the payout is final and executed in full, Then the withdrawal is Completed". AC-B1.3 becomes "Given the payout is proven never to execute, Then the redeemer gets one refund mint, or a new attempt is made (REQ-RF-1, REQ-RF-5, INV-8)". US-B2 is replaced by US-B5: "As anyone holding a bridged asset on the issuing network, I redeem it to the backend I name."

    • AC-B5.1 Given a redemption, Then its value is destroyed in the same call, and its reference and instructions (or their commitment) are recorded in order (REQ-OT-1, REQ-HN-7).
    • AC-B5.2 Given the same reference again, Then it is refused (INV-2).

  • CND-ReserveShort keeps its text with "the origin network's treasury" replaced by "the asset's reserve".

A7 — Payout instructions: inline for network backends, a commitment for attested ones (br-redeem-instructions)

  • Add REQ-RQ-9. A redemption MUST carry its payout instructions inline when the backend is public (a destination address), and only a commitment (a hash of the instructions) when the backend is attested. The bridge MUST accept instructions relayed for a commitment only when they hash to it.
  • REQ-PV-1 applies unchanged: no personal data on any network.

A8 — Recipients are responsible for receiving (br-recipients, chat, 2026-10-11)

  • REQ-HN-4 is withdrawn.
  • Add REQ-IN-9. The bridge MUST NOT check whether a destination on the issuing network can receive the asset. A mint the network refuses because of its destination holds CND-DestinationRefused and mints nothing. Past the route's deadline it goes to Needs-Review.
  • CND-DestinationRefused becomes: "The issuing network refused a mint because of its destination; nothing was minted."

A9 — No direct delivery into a ledger in V0 (br-kreivo-hop)

  • REQ-IN-5 becomes: "A deposit's destination is an address on the issuing network. Delivering into a ledger is the requester's, from its own account."
  • REQ-HN-6 is withdrawn. REQ-LG-1 becomes: "The bridge MUST NOT call a ledger's contract, hold a ledger's keys, accounts or authority, or report outcomes to it. Value reaches a ledger through the requester, and leaves it through the requester's redemption." REQ-LG-2 is withdrawn.
  • US-A3 and its criteria are withdrawn.
  • OQ-10 and OQ-13 are closed by this item.

A10 — Conversion on a protocol venue; no custodial venue in V0 (br-dex)

  • REQ-CV-2 becomes: "A conversion on a deposit MUST be executed by the bridge at a protocol venue, as one transaction whose output lands in the reserve. The deposit is then minted against that output, at its observed amount, under every rule of §5.5. The deposit records the convertible arrival and the converted output, with both amounts."
  • REQ-CV-3 becomes: "A withdrawal MAY name a convertible token. The bridge converts the reserve token at a protocol venue, in the payout transaction or just before it, and pays the beneficiary the output. The delivered amount is reported with both amounts."
  • Add DEF-8: "Custodial venues, such as an exchange, for conversion or for delivery to another network. V0 uses protocol venues only (br-dex)."
  • REQ-RB-6's custodial bound goes with the withdrawn REQ-RB-* (A3). REQ-VN-3 keeps only its first sentence. REQ-VN-5 is withdrawn.
  • OQ-9 is closed by this item.

What this costs, stated plainly

  • Attested assets rest on a requester's word. For COPM, MXNB and BRLB, the ramp's reports are the observations. The chain bounds how much can be minted. It cannot tell a real payment from a reported one.
  • Burning at once uses the mint authority for refunds. That is one refund per redemption, within the window. Escrow would have avoided it; the owner chose simplicity.
  • No direct delivery into aledgent in V0. Every deposit for aledgent passes through the requester's account: one more hop, and one more party holding value briefly.

Carried to the plan

  • One contract per bridge, in a dedicated ContractsStore community. kreivo#512 gives mint and burn, and the community account is Issuer and Admin.
  • Asset table: bUSDC (Polygon, native USDC), bEURC (Base, EURC), bBTC (BNB Chain, BTCB). COPM, MXNB and BRLB are attested through Swaps. Locations and decimals are open; see the form.
  • DEX venues: Uniswap v3 (Polygon) and Aerodrome (Base); CCTP for USDC deliveries to other networks.
  • The reserve book is a double-entry store inside the core.
