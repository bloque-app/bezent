# bezent brand assets

Copied on 2026-10-12 from the aledgent session's exports (the Bloque open-source family design). Names and
marks belong to Pagos Cube LLC and are not licensed with any code; a trademark check on bezent is pending.

Tokens: sky `#1f7bff` (bezent), ink `#0d0c17`, paper `#fbfaff`, Bloque violet full stop `#6a3cff` (on light) /
`#a78bfa` (on dark), muted `#6d6982` / `#9a96ad`. Type: Familjen Grotesk (display), Geist (body), Geist Mono
(code). Don't recolour, stretch or tile the symbol; don't rely on colour alone.

## Usage (final marks, confirmed 2026-10-12; identical to the files here)

- Clear space: 1 module around the symbol, 2 around lockups (module = one block + one gap).
- Sizes: full 5×5 symbol from 24 px; the 32 px art at 17–23 px; the 16 px favicon at ≤16 px. Horizontal lockup ≥24 px
  tall; stacked ≥72 px.
- Pages: `tools/md2artifact.py` inlines `lockup.svg` / `lockup-dark.svg` at 34 px tall (≈14 px = 2 modules below).
